# ADR-NNN: Short title naming the decision

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related:** issues, pull requests, other ADRs

## Context

The problem and the forces at play: requirements, constraints and facts that may change. Write it so someone who joins the project later understands why a decision was needed.

## Decision

What we do, in full sentences ("We use…"). Include the details others must follow: versions, file locations, conventions.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option:** why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder: the risks we accept and the follow-up work. Include the negative consequences, not only the positive ones.
